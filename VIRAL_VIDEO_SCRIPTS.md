## ⚡ The 21–30s Viral Formula (Zoe Cole Teardown)
1. **Visual Contrast:** Impossible aesthetics (striking ice-blue eyes, long silver hair, statuesque height) placed in everyday environments (café, street walk, home bedroom).
2. **Seamless Loop:** The video begins and ends on a neutral gaze so viewers watch it 2–3 times without realizing it looped (spiking algorithmic retention).
3. **No Fluff / Minimal Speech:** Hypnotic ambient audio or a short 1-line breathy hook. Let the visual presence do the selling.
4. **Engagement Bait:** An intriguing caption that sparks comment debates without ever breaking character or saying "I am AI".
