## 🛠️ 3. The 100% Free Production Workflow (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Master Face Generation
* Generate your initial master face reference portraits (saved in `/public/assets/character/`).
* Choose one primary high-resolution frontal shot as your **Master Anchor Face** (`anchor_face.png`).

### Step 2: 100% Free Unlimited Face Consistency
When you need Vespera in new poses, unboxings, or environments:
1. Open **[HuggingFace InstantID Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/InstantX/InstantID)** or **[PuLID Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/yanze/PuLID)** (both 100% free, no login or credit card required).
2. Upload `anchor_face.png` into the Identity Image box.
3. Paste any of the 10 Scene Prompts below into the prompt box.
4. Hit **Generate** — it renders the exact same face onto the new body, outfit, and background with 100% likeness.

### Step 3: Animate Face & Generate Video (Zero-Cost Talking Avatar)
1. Open **[LivePortrait on HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/spaces/KwaiVGI/LivePortrait)** (100% free).
2. Upload your generated Vespera image.
3. Upload a driving video or audio clip (recorded voice from ElevenLabs).
4. LivePortrait realistically maps natural blinks, head tilts, and mouth sync with zero distortion.

### Step 4: Voiceover (ElevenLabs Free Tier)
1. Use ElevenLabs Voice Design to create a calm, warm, articulate feminine voice.
2. Generate 30–60 second script voiceovers for your reels.

### Step 5: Final Viral Reel Assembly (CapCut Desktop & Canva Pro via GitHub Student Pack)
1. Layer Vespera's animated video or cinematic b-roll with the audio track.
2. In CapCut, click **Auto-Captions** → select trending bold caption style.
3. Add gentle sound effects (whooshes, clicks, chime upon revealing products).
4. Include call-to-action: *"Comment 'AURA' and my bot will DM you the exact link."*
